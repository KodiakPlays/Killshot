## Best order of steps (from the runs)

1. Scan it with ACTIVE SCAN. Torpedoes need a scan or a lock. Hit reports name the side and the damage only once it is scanned.
2. Go to its nearer side, square on (within 35°), at the range you fight from.
3. Turn, then stop. Hold Q or E until the slide stops.
4. Set the four switches before sighting.
5. Sight, lock, let the ship turn itself, fire when the nose stops. Each shot pushes you 24 units back. Nothing to correct.
6. Torpedoes at a moving ship: from 2,000 or closer.
7. Keep at least two PDC groups on. At x5, four.
8. Don't fire from past DRADIS: no lock, no torpedoes, no report.
