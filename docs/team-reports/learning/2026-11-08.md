## 1. Each level

### 1 · FLIGHT DRILL

- **Job:** fly the ship. One job. This is the best-built level. A note gives one step at a time, and the game checks each step before the next.
- **Briefing:** clear. Each note says the key and what "done" looks like.
- **Needs first:** nothing.
- **New:** P, key 3 and the ENG selector (DRAW, STANDBY, VENT), W and S, A and D, Q and E, zoom and the SCALE box, CHECKPOINT bearings on the alarm strip, DRADIS, ACTIVE SCAN and the cone dial, ROCK AHEAD, and stopping.
- **Time:** about 5 minutes at x1 (my sum: about 8,800 units of flying at 25 to 50 units a second, plus clicks).
- **Where a new player gets stuck:**
  - Step 1 wants ENG on STANDBY at 1 to 3 units. ENG fills one unit a second, so a slow clicker goes past 3. The note says how to fix it (VENT), so this is fine.
  - Steps 6 to 10 share one ring, at 600, -5,300, north of the HEADING ring. The ship keeps flying east while you do the slides, zoom and power steps, so it moves away from that ring. Flying into it early doesn't count until step 10. (My reading of the ring positions, not played.)
  - Step 13 (ROCK FIELD) is about 4,700 units in a straight line, about 95 seconds at full speed. Steps 11 and 12 fill some of it. Probably near or over the draft 30-second dead-time limit. Player 1 should check.
- **Not taught here, but needed in level 2:** ORDERS, gas clouds, the signal box, the red bar for a heard ship, and the time rate keys.

### 2 · COLD RUN

- **Job:** sneak past the Garuda to the jump point with weapons off. One job, and the briefing says it in its first line.
- **Needs first:** power, helm, DRADIS. FLIGHT DRILL teaches all three.
- **New:** a live enemy that hunts and fires, gas clouds, hiding behind rocks, your own signal, and the jump point.
- **Time:** about 51,500 units along the S. At full speed (50 a second) that is about 17 minutes at x1, or about 3.5 minutes at x5. At the quiet speed of ENG 1 (25 a second) it is about 34 minutes at x1 (my sums). The Garuda starts at the jump point end and flies toward you at 22 a second. Both moving, you would meet after about 11 minutes at x1, in the top lane (my sum, if neither stops).
- **Where a new player gets stuck:**
  - **Briefing step 2, "click the alert once, from C3 to C2", does nothing here.** C2 releases weapons and flight ops (`OrdersSystem.cs`), and both are off in this level. A player learns a step with no effect. INTERCEPT's briefing already leaves it out.
  - **"Watch the Garuda's path"** comes before you can hear it. It starts about 20,000 units away, three lanes over, behind two rock walls. Nothing shows for minutes.
  - **The red bar on the DRADIS circle is never explained here.** WEAPONS FREE (level 3) explains it. In COLD RUN it is the first thing that tells you the Garuda is near.
  - **"Less power means less signal" doesn't say where to look.** The SIGNAL box on the board shows it, but no level names it.
  - **Nothing tells you when you have been found.** In WOLF HUNT the strip says REINFORCEMENTS INBOUND. Here the first sign is being shot at. For a level whose job is "don't be found", the player can't tell when they failed.
  - **The bottom lane is about 14,500 units with no contact.** At x1 that is about 5 minutes of flying (my sum). Over the 30-second dead-time draft, unless the player uses x5. No briefing mentions the rate keys.

### 3 · WEAPONS FREE

- **Job:** destroy a still Garuda with the railgun. One goal, but it is really three jobs: scan, lock and fire. The owner already agreed to split it (FTUE_PLAN §0, "Splitting WEAPONS FREE").
- **Briefing:** clear, nine numbered steps.
- **Needs first:** power and turning (FLIGHT DRILL), DRADIS and ACTIVE SCAN (FLIGHT DRILL).
- **New:** WEP power, the C2 alert (it matters here), the red bar, aiming the scan cone at it, the gun cones, SIGHT, SLEW TO, MAG, the lock, LOAD, ANGLE, BEARING, ARM, and the lever.
- **Time:** unknown. The Garuda is 1,500 units away and still, so no travel. FTUE_PLAN counts about 19 actions to the first shot. How many hits kill it is Player 1's to find.
- **Where a new player gets stuck:**
  - **The lock is short.** It takes 10 seconds to fill and holds 10 seconds (`Asura.asset`). The README's "new" player takes 8 seconds to arm a shot. That leaves 2 seconds. The gun's bar also turns at only 3° a second, so it may not reach the Garuda before the lock drops. Player 1 should check how often a new player loses the lock before firing.
  - Step 9 says "If the Garuda has turned, scan again first". This Garuda never moves. Harmless, but it is noise.
  - The power step leaves ENG off, and step 6 says turn with A and D. That works: the ship turns at the ENG 1 rate with no ENG. But FLIGHT DRILL taught "ENG gives turn rate", so a player may think they can't turn.

### 4 · NO PEEKING

- **Job:** hit the Garuda once using only POINT and RULER. One job. The briefing is clear that a scan or a lock loses.
- **Needs first:** flying (level 1), the red bar and the firing switches (level 3).
- **New:** POINT, RULER, TRACK, and a moving target. The Garuda is 4,000 units away, moving west at 8 a second.
- **Time:** about 3 to 4 minutes at x1 (my sum: about 2,000 units to get it into sight, a 60-second wait between POINTs, then aim and fire).
- **Where a new player gets stuck:**
  - **The sight leads you into the loss.** Step 7 says SIGHT and SLEW TO. The sight then says ZOOM IN, and once the Garuda fits it says LOCKING (`PeriscopePanel.cs`). The lock fills by itself while the Garuda fits the sight (`WeaponSystem.cs`). A player who just learned MAG in WEAPONS FREE will do what the sight says, and lose. This is from the code, not played. Why does this level ask for SIGHT at all?
  - **POINT and RULER are new and never shown.** The briefing says "Put a POINT on the Garuda", but not that the tools are on the left rail.
  - **"A minute later, put another"** is 60 seconds of waiting. Over the dead-time draft. The Garuda moves 480 units in that minute. Would 30 seconds (240 units) still give a good course? Player 1 could check.

### 5 · FINDERS KEEPERS

- **Job:** capture the Garuda. One job. The briefing says destroying it loses.
- **Needs first:** scan, lock and fire (level 3).
- **New:** AUX power, the HANGAR, interceptors on DISABLE, doors, the six stage switches, the launch lever, assault boats on SECURE.
- **Time:** unknown. The Garuda is still, 1,500 units away. Player 1.
- **Where a new player gets stuck:**
  - **"They stop it", but it is already stopped.** The Garuda's cruise speed in this level is 0. The boats need only an open side and a nearly still ship (`HangarSystem.cs`). The interceptor step teaches the launch, but its effect can't be seen. The list row says "STOP IT, THEN SEND THE MARINES", too.
  - **"Stop once one side is open."** The briefing doesn't say where to see a side's armour. The boats do say NO OPEN SIDE while they wait.
  - **Boarding is a dice roll** (marines against defenders, in `HangarSystem.cs`). If it fails the strip says BOARDING REPELLED. The briefing doesn't say what to do then.

### 6 · INTERCEPT

- **Job:** reach the jump point and use the Garuda's radio to keep clear of it. One job.
- **Briefing:** clear and complete. It drops COLD RUN's needless C2 step.
- **The MISSIONS list row is wrong.** It reads "DECODE ITS RADIO MESSAGE. FLY THERE. FIND IT ON DRADIS." That was the old goal. The briefing now says the opposite: keep clear of it. A player who picks the level from the row will fly toward the Garuda.
- **Needs first:** everything in COLD RUN.
- **New:** COMMS: band switch, tuning knob, LOCK, the four dials. A message comes 15 seconds in, then about once a minute. It stays on air 60 seconds. It gives the Garuda's heading, speed and bearing in eight directions (`CommsSystem.cs`).
- **Time:** the same distance as COLD RUN, about 17 minutes at x1 at full speed (my sum). The radio fills COLD RUN's empty bottom lane with work, which is good.
- **Where a new player gets stuck:** the comms work is four new controls against a 60-second clock, and nothing teaches them earlier. As the teaching level for comms, that is fair.

### 7 · WOLF HUNT

- **Job:** the full mission. Find the Garuda, destroy or capture it, reach the jump point. The briefing is a list of tips, not steps. That is right for a final test.
- **Needs first:** everything above, plus things no level teaches (next section).
- **Time:** unknown. You start about 16,000 units from the Garuda's box. The jump point opens 5,000 units in from the far edge. Travel alone is several minutes at x1 (my sum). Player 1.
