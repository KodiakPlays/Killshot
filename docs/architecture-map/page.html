    The three main scripts

    What happens every 0.42 seconds

    The game's rules update about two and a half times a second. Ships still glide smoothly on screen in between. Each update goes in this order:

    1. BoardManager starts the update. How much game time passes depends on the speed the player picked: x0.25, x1 or x5. When the game is paused, nothing happens.
    2. The mission timer goes up.
    3. Our ship updates its parts one after another: power, orders, steering, the battle space edge, shots in flight, fighters and incoming fire. Then come repairs and fires, launches, the weapons charge, the lock, the guns, the sensors and the radio.
    4. TacticalWorld updates every other ship. Each ship's EnemyBrain decides what to do, and then the ship moves. After that the game checks for crashes and works out where each ship is compared to ours.
    5. QuestSystem checks whether the mission has been won or lost.
    6. Every panel on the board redraws itself.
