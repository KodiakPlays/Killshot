# Milk Run — station approach

The player receives orders from the battle group commander and carries them out at the controls. Each radio cue is a single incoming transmission. Command says “ping” on the radio. The sensor panel reads ACTIVE SCAN.

## Checkpoint 14 · Take point

**COMMAND** · We'll go round the rocks. You take the short way through.

## Checkpoint 16 · Station in sight

**COMMAND** · Station traffic control isn't answering. Ping the station.

**SENSORS** · Switch ACTIVE SCAN on. Hold the cone on the station until its name appears.

## Checkpoint 17 · Station return

**COMMAND** · Hull breach confirmed. Escape pods in the wreckage. DRADIS has a faint contact. Increase gain.

**SENSORS** · Press 3 and bring SEN to 4. Press 2 and raise GAIN to 4.

## Checkpoint 18 · Contact east

**COMMAND** · Contact east of the station. Ping it for identification.

**SENSORS** · Keep ACTIVE SCAN on. Move the cone from the station to the red contact. Hold it there until the ship's name appears.

## Checkpoint 19 · Pursuit order

**COMMAND** · Pirate corvette, moving away. Pursue immediately, Captain. Torpedoes only. We'll recover the pods.

**ORDERS** · Set Condition 2 to release weapons.
